Helpful details,
before your visit.

How do I request an appointment?

Choose a preferred date in the calendar, then prepare your WhatsApp message. The practice confirms the appointment directly.

Do I need an account?

No. You can call, use WhatsApp and prepare a request without signing up.

Where is the practice?

At 48 Avenue Habib Bourguiba, 2nd floor, office 2.2, Ariana. The Google Maps button opens the practice listing.

Are my details stored?

The site prepares your message in your browser without saving your details. It is sent only when you confirm in WhatsApp.

The map loads from Google Maps only when you open it.
